Early Life

Imran Khan was born on 5 October 1952 in Lahore, Pakistan. He belonged to a Pashtun family and lost his father early. He studied at Aitchison College, then at Oxford University, where he developed his cricketing skills and leadership.

🎂 Born: 5 Oct 1952, Lahore
🎓 Education: Oxford University
🧬 Family: Pashtun heritage
🏏 Debut: 1971 vs England

Cricket Journey

Imran Khan made his Test debut in 1971 and went on to become one of the greatest all‑rounders in cricket history. He led Pakistan to its first and only World Cup victory in 1992.

Political Journey

After retiring from cricket, Imran Khan entered politics in 1996 by founding the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI). He focused on anti‑corruption, justice, and social welfare. After years of struggle, he became the 22nd Prime Minister of Pakistan in 2018.
